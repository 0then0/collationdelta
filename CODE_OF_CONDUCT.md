# Code of Conduct

CollationDelta welcomes contributors and users regardless of experience, background
or identity. This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews and other
project-managed spaces, and when someone represents the project publicly.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, give constructive technical feedback and assume good intent while
remaining open to correction. Discuss code and evidence rather than making personal
judgments. Respect other people's time, boundaries and privacy. Accept that reasonable
people can disagree about an implementation.

Harassment, discriminatory or sexualized remarks, threats, personal attacks, repeated
unwanted contact and deliberate disruption are not acceptable. Do not publish private
information or encourage others to target a participant.

## Reporting and enforcement

The repository maintainer, [@0then0](https://github.com/0then0), handles project conduct
reports. For a matter that can be discussed publicly, describe the concern factually
without exposing personal information. For a private matter, use the [private contact request form](https://github.com/0then0/collationdelta/issues/new?template=private_contact.yml)
without posting incident details. If no private contact channel is available,
use GitHub's platform reporting tools for abuse occurring on GitHub.

Maintainers may remove or edit disruptive content, request a change in behavior,
restrict participation or ban participants, depending on severity and repetition.
Reports should be handled with respect for the privacy and safety of those involved.
Maintainers are subject to the same expectations. GitHub's own rules and reporting
processes also apply to activity on its platform.
